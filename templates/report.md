# Meeting report

The core document. Everything later (opinions, options, decision, cable) is written from it, so it must be
complete and every line must cite its segment.

```
<Body>, <meeting name> — <subject>                     (title: the meeting's own name, then the subject)
Reported by: <Counsellor>   ·   Profile <version>   ·   [low confidence: <reason>, if the record is weak]

Key points for the Mission            two to four points: what matters for the State, each cited
Issues                                what was discussed [E1]
Briefers (council meetings only)      who briefed, and the gist [E2]
Introductions                         who introduced which draft (<symbol>) [E3]
Positions                             each delegation's distinct position, grouped by draft [E4]
Group statements                      EVERY statement on behalf of a group, always kept [E5]
Agreement / disagreement              where delegations converged or split [E4][E6]
Outcome                               what was adopted, voted, deferred [E7]
Votes                                 <symbol>: adopted, <n> in favour, <n> against, <n> abstentions [E7]
```

## Invented example (extract)

> **Committee on Shared Waters, 14th meeting — Draft on river data sharing**
>
> **Key points.** The draft on sharing river data (L.4) was adopted without a vote [E5]. The Coastal Group asked
> that data be shared "within thirty days of collection" [E3].
>
> **Group statements.** Arvenia, on behalf of the Coastal Group, supported L.4 and the thirty-day rule [E3].
>
> **Outcome.** L.4 adopted without a vote [E5].

## What it must carry

- A citation on every line, pointing to a segment where the fact is found.
- Every group statement, every introduction, every vote on a whole text.
- Tallies exactly as the chair announced them; "tally unclear in the record" if they cannot be read.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Figures or quotations not in the cited segment; a quotation that is not word for word.
- Presiding officers or the secretariat listed as delegations.
- Content of a draft that was not read out in the meeting.
- Background the model "knows" from elsewhere.
- Naming a delegation in "agreement" that never spoke in the cited segment.
