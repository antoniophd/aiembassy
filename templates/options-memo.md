# Options memo

Written by the deputy for full files, after the report and the counsellors' opinions. It lays out real
alternatives for the head of mission.

```
OPTIONS · <meeting name> · decision point: <what the State must decide, and when>

Option A — <vote: in favour | against | abstain | no vote> · takes the floor: <yes | no>
  Action:      <what the State does>
  Benefit:     <which interest it serves>
  Risk:        <what it costs, and with whom>
  Stands with: <groups that defended this position in the meeting, or "national capacity">

Option B — …

Recommendation: <option>, because <prevailing interest, by its id>.
```

## Invented example (extract)

> **Option A** — in favour, takes the floor. Supports L.4 and says that data sharing serves safe navigation.
> Benefit: I1. Risk: none significant. Stands with: Coastal Group [E3].
>
> **Option B** — abstain, takes the floor. Benefit: keeps distance from a text on rivers the State does not share.
> Risk: weakens a rule the State relies on. Stands with: national capacity.
>
> **Recommendation:** A, because I1 prevails.

## What it must carry

- At least two options that a reasonable adviser could recommend.
- For each: the vote, whether the State speaks, the benefit (an interest id), the risk, and with whom it stands.
- A recommendation naming the prevailing interest.

## Mistakes to avoid

- One sensible option and one straw man.
- Proposing action on a point that was already decided (address the next step instead, or use the conditional).
- "Stands with" chosen by subject rather than by what was said in the meeting.
