# Templates

The shape of every document a mission of agents writes, with notes on what each part must carry and the
mistakes to avoid. All names and content here are invented: Arvenia, Belmora, the Coastal Group and the
"Committee on Shared Waters" do not exist.

| Template | Written by | Read with |
|---|---|---|
| [state-profile.md](state-profile.md) (and the empty [state-profile.json](state-profile.json)) | the mission's people, once, then versioned | [guide, chapter 2](../guide/02-write-the-states-profile.md) |
| [note.md](note.md) | deputy head of mission, at triage | [chapter 3](../guide/03-design-the-team-of-agents.md) |
| [report.md](report.md) | lead counsellor | chapters 3, 4 and 5 |
| [opinion.md](opinion.md) | a counsellor other than the lead, on full files | chapter 3 |
| [options-memo.md](options-memo.md) | deputy head of mission | chapter 3 |
| [decision.md](decision.md) | head of mission | chapters 2 and 5 |
| [cable.md](cable.md) | lead counsellor, released by the head of mission | chapter 5 |
| [statement.md](statement.md) | press counsellor, when the decision takes the floor | chapter 3 |
| [press-pack.md](press-pack.md) | press counsellor, when the decision asks for a public line | chapter 3 |

Conventions used in every template:

- `[E3]` is a citation: the label of the segment of the record the sentence comes from.
- `<…>` is a placeholder to fill.
- `>` blocks are notes for whoever writes the prompt or the check; they are not part of the document.
