# Decision

Taken by the head of mission on every full file. It is the State's position on the subject and is recorded, so
the next meeting on the same subject starts from it.

```
DECISION · <meeting name> · Profile <version>

Position:        <one or two sentences: what the State does and says>
Option chosen:   <A | B | …>, as offered in the memo (vote and floor unchanged)
Why:             <the prevailing interest (id) and why it prevails>
Rejected:        <each other option, and why>
Interests:       <ids>          Red lines considered: <ids>, not crossed
Stands with:     <groups whose stance in this meeting was the same> [cite each], or "national capacity"
Group positions: <each known group that spoke: same | different | not addressed> [cite]
Change:          first | maintains | revises  (against the last position on this subject; if revises, what changed)
Reopens if:      <an event the body can produce: a vote, an amendment, a new draft>
Press:           none | statement | explanation of vote | press note
```

## Invented example (extract)

> **Position.** The State votes in favour of L.4 and says that shared river data makes navigation safer.
> **Why.** I1 (safe sea lanes) is served by the text; no red line is engaged.
> **Stands with.** Coastal Group [E3]. **Change.** first. **Reopens if** the draft is amended to limit data
> sharing to members of a basin.

## What it must carry

- An option that was offered, unchanged; interests and red lines that exist in the profile.
- "Stands with" that follows from the meeting: groups with the same stance, each cited, or national capacity.
- A change mark against the previous position on the same subject, decided by the State's line (its vote, stance
  and conditions), not by who it coincided with this time.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Choosing a position to join a group.
- "Reopens if" written as something the State would do.
- A position that contradicts the chosen vote (taking no side while voting yes).
