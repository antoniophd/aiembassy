# Note (procedural meeting)

Written at triage when a meeting needs no report. A few lines; its value is that every meeting leaves a trace.

```
NOTE · <body>, <meeting name> · <date>
Written by: Deputy head of mission

<One to three sentences: what the meeting did [E1]. Whether anything touched the State's interests: usually "No
interest engaged.">
```

## Invented example

> NOTE · Committee on Shared Waters, 12th meeting · 3 March
>
> The Committee adopted its agenda and elected Belmora to the vice-chair [E1]. No debate. No interest engaged.

## What it must carry

- What was done, with a citation.
- Whether any interest was engaged (if one was, it is not a note: send it to a report).

## Mistakes to avoid

- A note for a meeting that adopted or voted a text on a subject the State follows.
- Commentary ("a routine but useful meeting"): the note records, it does not judge.
- Anything not cited.
